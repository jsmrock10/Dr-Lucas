04 · Proposed co-build operating model

Five-agent collaboration protocol.

These agents are planned workstreams, not currently running autonomous integrations. Academic participants are invited to approve scientific interpretation and use of their name independently.

Research Evidence Agent · Bibliography; methods validity; reproducibility packet; professor review gate
Buyer Discovery Agent · Interviews and pain quantification; direct buyer approval before outreach
MVP Engineering Agent · Synthetic fixtures, code and deterministic tests; no live system access initially
Commercial & GTM Agent · Competitor matrix, pilot pricing, proposals and procurement checklist
Assurance Agent · Data/IP review, safety, audit, economics and professor/buyer sign-offs

Joint governance & deliverables

Week 1: research memo + two buyer interviews + no IP claims.

Week 2: structured sample + acceptance criteria approved by buyer.

Week 3: deterministic prototype test + security review.

Week 4: demonstration + paid pilot decision + rights discussion.

Task queue is local to this browser; not shared with professors or connected to email.

05 · Go-to-market

Sell the quantified problem, then expand.

Target commercial wedge

Head of export development; trade finance

Discovery: quantify volume, error/exception frequency, average handling cost, and incumbent implementation friction.

Pilot: one site / team, one dataset, one metric, named sign-off owner, documented rollback.

Expansion: add integrations, adjacent sites, analytics and optional hardware only if proof supports it.

Proposed adapters: WITS/Comtrade subject to licenses, exporter CRM.

Editable pricing economics

Partner share is not a negotiated term; zero is a placeholder only. Gross profit is fee less direct costs and partner share, before tax, overhead and acquisition expense.
